Our Story

About Shreshtha Makhana Flavours

Shreshtha Makhana Flavours brings delicious, crunchy and flavourful makhana (fox nuts) to Indian households, offices and college hostels — without the guilt of deep frying.

Makhana has been part of Indian kitchens for generations, prized for being light, protein-rich and naturally gluten-free. We wanted to take that everyday superfood and give it the flavour and presentation of a modern, premium snack — so we roast every batch in small quantities, season it by hand with spice blends drawn from India's chaat stalls, tandoors and pickle jars, and seal it fresh in hygienic, resealable packaging.

From a lightly salted classic to a fiery Schezwan, every Shreshtha Makhana flavour is built around one promise: real crunch, real flavour, and a snack you can feel good about reaching for — at your desk, on a road trip, or in front of a movie.
